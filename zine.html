Outpost L7


Our Zine

Outpost L7 is our science fiction creative works zine. We accept anything from, but not limited to...

  • Illustration
  • Collage
  • Prose & Poetry
  • Short Fiction
  • Articles
  • Comics

Process

Following our informational night on 22/10, submissions are open until 27 December, 2026!

We'll hold one workshop night a month for people to come together to work on their projects - feel free to discuss in our servers as well.
Send your submissions via this form [coming soon]. Once we've reviewed all submissions, we'll send you an email in the new year!

We will distribute physical zines for sale in the new year. People who have submitted approved works will receive a free physical copy. All digital copies are free!

Submission FAQ

What's the format?

The physical zine will be in black&white, approximately A5 size, printed on heavyweight paper.

What's allowed?

We allow any visual or written creative works in the physical zine, and allow audiovisual mediums to be linked in the digital zine.

  • Depictions of nudity and graphic violence are reviewed on a case-by-case basis
  • Fanworks are welcome!
  • All digital art must be submitted as .SVG
  • All physical art and collages must be scanned as .png or .jpg
  • Photography must be .png
  • Written works, with some exceptions, may not go over 5k words
